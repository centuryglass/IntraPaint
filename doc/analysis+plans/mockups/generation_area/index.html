Try your 1024×768 routine

  1. Click the Full frame chip (or press Q) and do a whole-image pass.
  2. Press Q to flip back to the 768² square. The resolution follows the area, with no extra clicks.
  3. With the selection brush, paint over a detail. The area follows the selection, and the crop hugs it.
  4. Right-click a spot you want the model to see. Right-drag to add a whole region. Press F4 and the pins clear.
  5. Shift+scroll anywhere over the canvas or navigation panel to change padding without leaving the current tool.
  6. Paint a selection wider than 768px to see the overflow behaviors.
